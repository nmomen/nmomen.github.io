About

Nurul Momen is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at OsloMet – Oslo Metropolitan University, where he is Academic Coordinator for the Master’s Programme in Cyber Security. He is also Associate Professor II at the University of Oslo.

Before joining OsloMet in 2025, he spent five years at Blekinge Institute of Technology in Sweden, first as Lecturer and then as Assistant Professor, where he directed two engineering programmes in computer and IT security. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Karlstad University in 2020, and holds master’s degrees from Technische Universität Berlin and the University of Trento through the EIT Digital Master School in cyber security.

Research

His work sits where security, privacy and people meet: measuring what apps actually do with personal data, designing consent that people can meaningfully act on, and building privacy and security into systems, including AI. His doctoral research introduced ways to measure and expose the data access behaviour of mobile apps, and his later work extends to partial consent, digital sovereignty and collaborative AI engineering.

Bachelor’s and master’s theses

Ten theses at Blekinge Institute of Technology and Karlstad University (2019–2023), on topics including partial consent on Android, privacy indicators, cookies and dark patterns under the GDPR, HTTP security headers in Swedish government agencies, Tor traffic analysis and onion-service deanonymisation, and operating system security in embedded systems. He also supervised student software projects, including KAUdroid, a tool for visualising how Android apps use permissions.
